## Negative control: does an XOR-salt change which flows are downgraded?

CRC is affine over GF(2), so a salt shifts every slot by one constant and collisions are unchanged. Prediction (pre-registered): outcomes identical to the unkeyed run at the same clock.

- Clock varied: 30 of 30 runs have per-packet outcomes identical to the unkeyed run.
- Clock fixed at grid 0: 30 of 30 salts give identical outcomes.
