## Reading

The pre-registered decision rules are applied to the recovery column only. A defence's benign gain (no-attack column) is shown apart from its mitigation so that a fix which helps benign traffic is not mistaken for attack mitigation. The attacker is the analytical holder abstraction; the emulator-to-switch validation of every defence remains owed.
