## Exploratory baseline: double the table (not pre-registered in G6; baseline 5 of the paper plan)

Primary day, oracle gate, clocks 0 to 9 (10 draws). The same number of holders (10% of 65,536 slots = 5% of 131,072) attacks a table of twice the size; D4 keeps the capacity at 65,536 slots. Downgraded benign flows:

| design | slots | no attack | under fill | excess |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| undefended | 65,536 | 572 | 1,219 | 647 |
| D4 | 65,536 | 52 | 216 | 164 |
| undefended | 131,072 | 291 | 642 | 351 |

Excess under fill: D4 164 flows, doubled table 351 flows; total downgraded under fill: D4 216, doubled table 642. D4 costs a second hash and a register split at equal capacity; a larger table costs SRAM.

Reading: emulator results with an analytical holder abstraction. Deployability on Tofino-1 is unverified (`docs/lit/overnight_p4_feasibility.md`). The load-sweep row where R crosses zero bounds the claim: recovery of the attack's excess holds while benign occupancy is low and reverses when the table is heavily loaded, even though D4 still lowers the absolute number of downgraded flows under attack because of its benign gain.
